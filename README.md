# Restaurant templates (free, EN + ES)

21 templates for running one restaurant or a group: opening and closing checklists, temperature and cooling logs, cleaning schedules, prep and par sheets, an allergen matrix, onboarding and training plans, an SOP template, a recipe card, an inventory count sheet with variance, a food cost calculator, a prime cost tracker, a multi-unit scorecard and a new-location opening checklist. PDF, Excel (with working formulas) and Word, in English and Spanish, no sign-up.

Each template has a guide page on how to use it on a real shift and across several locations: **https://www.restaurantcodex.com/resources** (Spanish: https://www.restaurantcodex.com/es/resources). Guides on operations, SOPs, training, food safety and food cost: https://www.restaurantcodex.com/insights.

Temperatures follow the FDA Food Code 2022 (cold holding 41°F / 5°C or below, hot holding 135°F / 57°C or above, two-stage cooling). Allergen columns use the nine US major allergens including sesame (FASTER Act, 2023). Sources are cited on each guide page.
